About Steve Jobs

Steve Jobs was an American entrepreneur and technology visionary who co-founded Apple in 1976. He was born in 1955 and died in 2011 at the age of 56. Jobs played a major role in the development of products such as the Macintosh, iPod, iPhone and iPad. His approach to combining technology, simplicity and design had a major influence on the modern technology industry.

Key Contributions

  • Co-founded Apple Computer with Steve Wozniak
  • Helped popularize personal computers through the Macintosh
  • Led the development of the iPod and transformed digital music
  • Introduced the iPhone and changed the smartphone industry.
  • Helped make product design and user experience central to consumer technology

His impact on technology

Steve Jobs was not responsible for inventing every technology used in Apple's products. His major strength was bringing technology, design and user experience together into products that were understandable and attractive to ordinary people.

His work helped change how people interact with computers, music, phones and digital media. The philosophy of creating simple products with a strong focus on the user continues to influence technology companies today.

A famous idea

Jobs became known for presenting technology as something more than hardware. For him, the experience of using a product was just as important as the technology inside it.
